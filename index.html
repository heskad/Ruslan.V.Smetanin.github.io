[ 02 / CTB ASSAY WORKSPACE ]

From plate reader
to dose–response.

Two local-first tools for CellTiter-Blue workflows: normalize replicate fluorescence and plot viability, then prepare consistent dosing mixes from a drug stock.

RAW RFU → NORMALIZED VIABILITY

CTB graph builder

TOOL / 01

Paste one dose per row followed by replicate RFU values. The vehicle control is normalized to 100%; error bars show sample SD.

Dose [µM]nMean RFUCorrected RFUViability [%]SD [%]

Blank correction: RFUcorrected = RFU − blank. Normalization: viability = mean corrected sample / mean corrected vehicle control × 100.

STOCK → PLATE-READY MASTER MIX

Drug dose calculator

TOOL / 02

Calculate one dosing mix per concentration. By default, extra vehicle is added so every condition matches the highest-dose vehicle percentage.

Final [µM]Dosing mix [µM]Total mix [µL]Stock [µL]Extra vehicle [µL]Medium [µL]Vehicle final [%]pmol / well

Dosing mix concentration = final concentration × final well volume / dosing volume. Stock volume = dosing mix concentration × total mix volume / stock concentration.

Planning aid: confirm stock concentration, solvent tolerance, plate layout and assay-specific controls against your laboratory SOP before dosing cells.
